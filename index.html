Put your drawing into Desmos

  1. Click Make Desmos formulas and wait for the preview.
  2. In the preview, choose The whole clip, then click Copy formulas.
  3. Open the Desmos Graphing Calculator.
  4. Click an empty expression line and paste: Ctrl + V on Windows or ⌘ + V on Mac.
  5. If the graph looks blank, click the wrench, open Graph Settings, and enter the x/y-axis bounds shown below. These exact values account for the drawing width and your video’s aspect ratio.
  6. On the f slider, click either end number (it may show −10 and 10). Set the lower bound to 1, the upper bound to the number beside K, and the step to 1.
  7. Click the ▶ play button beside the f slider. Your clip will play from its first frame to its last.
